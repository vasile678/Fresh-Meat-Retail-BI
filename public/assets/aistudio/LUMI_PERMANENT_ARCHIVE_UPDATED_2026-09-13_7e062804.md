### Fresh Meat BI --- WK37 routing incident and permanent execution control

**Incident** A request for WK37 fresh-meat promotions was initially
handled as generic research instead of being routed directly through the
active **Fresh Meat Retail BI → WEEKLY --- RETAIL MEAT PROMOTIONS**
workflow. A follow-up changing the time window to **±3 days** should
have modified only that variable while preserving the active BI task.
The initial research was also stopped too early with an
insufficient-data conclusion; later execution demonstrated that a useful
BI report could be produced.

**Root cause** - incorrect domain/workflow routing; - insufficient
preservation of active-task state across follow-ups; - premature
research-sufficiency conclusion; - output validation should occur before
delivery.

**Permanent operational rule --- Fresh Meat BI Execution Gate** For any
Fresh Meat Retail request involving promotions, prices, competitors,
pork market, category performance, or closely related commercial
intelligence:

1.  Route implicitly through **Fresh Meat Retail BI** without requiring
    Vali to name the module.
2.  Select the appropriate existing BI workflow before
    research/execution.
3.  On follow-ups, preserve the active task and all unchanged
    constraints; modify only the variable explicitly changed by Vali.
4.  Before final output, validate material mappings: **retailer ×
    SKU/product × price × period × source/provenance**.
5.  If initial research is incomplete, do not conclude "insufficient
    data" prematurely. Expand/reformulate research when additional
    evidence can materially improve the requested report.
6.  Preserve evidence discipline: unverified competitors/products/prices
    remain **UNKNOWN / NOT CONFIRMED** rather than being inferred.
7.  This is an execution gate/control on the existing Meat Retail BI
    workflow, **not a new module**.

**Regression check** Future short prompts such as "promoții carne WK38",
"±3 zile", "doar porc", "adaugă Profi", or "compară cu WK37" must
preserve the Meat Retail BI workflow automatically and change only the
requested dimension.

**Decision** The correction is considered operationally successful only
when these regression prompts execute correctly without Vali needing to
remind LUMI to use Meat Retail BI.
